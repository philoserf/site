This is what to buy for Constructed and draft, as of January 2027. It covers only Magic's own worlds and leaves out the crossover (Universes Beyond) sets. For the formats themselves, see [Getting started with Magic in 2027 constructed and draft](/posts/getting-started-with-magic-in-2027-constructed-and-draft/).

## The short answer

Buy singles to build a constructed deck. Packs are a lottery, and buying the exact cards a decklist needs costs far less than opening packs to find them.

To draft or play sealed, buy Booster Packs, or pay the entry at a store event, where the packs are included. Booster Pack is Wizards' name for the draft pack from Nauctis: The Sunken Realm on; the 2024–2026 sets called theirs Play Boosters. To learn the game, buy the Foundations Beginner Box. Buy Collector Boosters only as collectibles: they hold the same cards in fancier finishes at five times the price.

## Which sets to buy

The sets from Magic's own worlds worth buying into, newest first:

- Nauctis: The Sunken Realm, prerelease January 29 to February 4, 2027, release February 5.
- Reality Fracture, released October 2, 2026.
- Secrets of Strixhaven, released April 24, 2026.
- Lorwyn Eclipsed, released January 23, 2026.
- Foundations, the core set for new players, legal in Standard until at least 2029.

Standard rotates on January 29, 2027, and drops Wilds of Eldraine, The Lost Caverns of Ixalan, Murders at Karlov Manor, Outlaws of Thunder Junction, Bloomburrow, and Duskmourn. Sealed product from those six is fine for drafting at the kitchen table, and often discounted, but their rares are no longer Standard cards. Every set in the list above stays: the three 2026 sets until the first set of 2029, and Foundations until at least 2029.

## The sets in brief

Foundations is the base everything else sits on. The three 2026 sets tell one continuous story: a group of Strixhaven students stumbles into Lorwyn, goes home to a Strixhaven in crisis, and ends up in the fight over Jace Beleren's plan to replace the Multiverse in Reality Fracture. Nauctis opens a new story.

- Foundations (November 15, 2024) is a core set with no story and no new mechanics, on purpose: about half reprints of proven cards and half new, all built on the evergreen keywords. It is the set behind the Beginner Box and the Starter Collection, which makes it the natural first set.
- Lorwyn Eclipsed is the creature-type set. Lorwyn and its dark mirror, Shadowmoor, bleed together. It introduced blight (put -1/-1 counters on a creature you control) and vivid (abilities that count your permanents' colors), and brought back kindred, changeling, convoke, persist, and hybrid mana. Lorwyn's eight creature types each anchor a two-color draft archetype; it is the set to pick if you like building around a tribe.
- Secrets of Strixhaven is the spells set. It returns to the magic university, where Jace's attempt to rewrite the past begins. It introduced prepare (a creature carrying a linked instant or sorcery you can cast while it stays prepared) and paradigm (Lessons you can recast for free once they have resolved), plus a mechanic for each of its five colleges. The colleges make five clear draft archetypes, which makes it one of the easier recent sets to learn drafting with.
- Reality Fracture is the event set that pays off the multi-year story: Jace builds the Echoverse, a mirrored reality meant to replace the Multiverse, and old allies fight to stop him. It introduced empower Jace (create a shared Jace planeswalker token, or add loyalty to it) and printed familiar planeswalkers beside their Echoverse twins. Its planeswalker theme makes it the most different of the 2026 sets to draft.
- Nauctis: The Sunken Realm is a new plane and the first set entirely underwater: sunken cities, two kingdoms at war, gods that command the currents. It opens a multi-year story arc.

## Ready-to-play decks

None of the recent sets has a ready-to-play constructed deck; Nauctis's only preconstructed decks are four Commander decks at $49.99. Outside Commander, Foundations has two starting points:

- Foundations Beginner Box ($29.99): about 200 cards in ten 20-card themed packets. Shuffle two packets together and you have a 40-card deck, so two people can learn and play straight out of the box.
- Foundations Starter Collection ($59.99): 350 or so cards, 3 Play Boosters, and 95 basic lands. It's a starting collection to build decks from, not a set of finished decks, and stock has thinned since its 2024 release.

## Buying singles

Look a card up on Scryfall for its price, then buy from TCGplayer, Card Kingdom, or a local store's singles case. The cheapest printing plays exactly like the fancy one. Before paying, check that the card comes from a set staying in Standard.

## Buying safely

Buy sealed product from a local store or a major retailer. Resealed and counterfeit packs turn up on marketplaces, especially in high-priced Collector Booster boxes.
