---
title: AI slop reflects human indifference, not machine failure
description: "There is a word for the flood of mediocre machine output: slop. The word blames the machine. But someone set the standard, read the result, and pressed publish."
date: 2026-11-28
---

"AI slop" locates the failure in the instrument rather than in the person directing it. A human started the work, set—or neglected to set—the standards, and accepted the result as fit to publish.

Making poor material is one act; deciding to publish it is another.

The problem is not that machines can produce mediocrity. Humans always have. Machines have cut the cost of producing and distributing it without any matching gain in human judgment.

AI slop is industrialized human indifference.
