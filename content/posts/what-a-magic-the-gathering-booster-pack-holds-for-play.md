From Nauctis: The Sunken Realm (February 5, 2027), the pack you draft, play sealed with, and open at a prerelease is the Booster Pack. Here is what it holds as game pieces: how many cards, at what rarity, and what that means for building a deck.

## The pack

A Booster Pack holds 14 cards: 13 from the set and its Special Guests, plus a basic land.

| Slot         | Cards | Rarity                           |
| ------------ | ----- | -------------------------------- |
| Commons      | 6     | Common                           |
| Seventh card | 1     | Common, or a Special Guests card |
| Uncommons    | 3     | Uncommon                         |
| Rare         | 1     | Rare or mythic rare              |
| Wildcard     | 1     | Uncommon, rare, or mythic rare   |
| Last card    | 1     | Common, rare, or mythic rare     |
| Land         | 1     | Basic land                       |

A token comes with it; it isn't a card you can put in a deck.

## What every pack guarantees

About one pack in 1,000 is a God Pack, every nonbasic card in it a rare or mythic rare; the guarantees below describe the rest.

- At least one rare. The rare slot always holds a rare or a mythic. The wildcard and the last card can hold more, so a pack has one to three.
- At least six commons and three uncommons. Commons and uncommons make up most of every Limited deck, and these slots fix how many each pack brings.
- A basic land, which you don't need. Basics are free at every draft and sealed event, so the land slot adds nothing to a deck.

Wizards hasn't published odds for the slots, so how often the wildcard and the last card come up rare is unknown.

## Special Guests

Special Guests are cards from outside the set, reprinted to sit in its packs. One can take the seventh card's place. They're legal in a Nauctis draft or sealed deck like any card you open, but they weren't designed for the set's Limited environment, so judge each one by what it does in your deck, not by its rarity.

## What a draft or sealed pool holds

- Draft. Each player opens three packs, so a pod of eight opens 24 packs and at least 24 rares, three per player. You make 42 picks and play 23 or so of them.
- Sealed. A Nauctis prerelease gives each player six Booster Packs, 84 cards with at least six rares, plus one extra rare or mythic. You build a 40-card deck from that pool.
