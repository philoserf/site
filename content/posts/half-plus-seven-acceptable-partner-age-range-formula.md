---
title: Half-plus-seven acceptable partner age range formula
description: "Formula for the acceptable partner age range at age n: lower bound is the greater of n/2 + 7 and a cultural minimum age, upper bound is 2n − 14."
date: 2026-03-13
math: true
aliases:
  - Dating Age Range Formula
---

For ages $n \geq n_{\min}$ (cultural), acceptable partner ages fall in $\left[\max\!\left(\frac{n}{2} + 7, n_{\min}\right), 2n - 14\right]$.
