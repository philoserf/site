Trump's presidency is a stress test. Guardrails everyone assumed were solid turn out to be conventions. The press, the courts, federalism—forced into open defense by a single administration's pressure.

People understand their values when threatened. So do societies. Stability hides decay; disruption exposes it.

But gains born of reaction depend on memory and follow-through. The same pressure that strengthens institutions damages trust and normalizes what it was meant to expose. And the cost of indirect progress never falls on those who caused it.

A country can improve because it was forced to look at itself. Whether that becomes renewal or only survival depends on what gets preserved once the pressure lifts.
