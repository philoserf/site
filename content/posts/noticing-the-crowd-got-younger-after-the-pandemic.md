---
title: Noticing the crowd got younger after the pandemic
description: "A short personal observation on aging: before the pandemic the surrounding crowd always seemed older, but after emerging from seclusion the crowd suddenly seemed younger."
date: 2026-03-12
aliases:
  - Aging Past the Crowd
---

For most of my life, the crowd was older than I was. Then came a pandemic. We went into seclusion, and when we came out, the crowd was younger.
