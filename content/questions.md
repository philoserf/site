I ask about a lot of things, and I'm still startled to find a subject I hadn't been asking about.

- What is thinking for?
- What does time actually do?
- What does it mean to belong?
- How should a curious person live?
- Is most ambition unresolved pain?
- Why do bad systems beat good people?
- Was Mehmed VI the last Roman emperor?
- Why does every age feel uniquely fast?
- What matters enough to justify refusal?
- How did tiddlywinks become competitive?
- Did medieval people love their children?
- Why does maintenance always feel optional?
- What would it mean to reenchant the world?
- Where does a self stop and the world start?
- What is light doing between here and there?
- [Have we lost agency, or did we ever have it?]({{< relref "agency-without-a-self-or-free-will" >}})
- Why does the burnt bit work only as an accent?
- [How do founding promises outlive their founders?]({{< relref "the-republic-as-something-each-generation-must-keep" >}})
- Is the Great Filter ahead of or behind humanity?
- Is everyone selling you something you don't need?
- What is worth doing if it isn't obviously useful?
- Can we know something is useful before we need it?
- How much of the world is projection mirrored back?
- Why do doors, windows, and mirrors seem so strange?
- What is the difference between knowing and understanding?
- Why does hitting off a mat differ from hitting off grass?
- Why does continuous improvement so often make things worse?
- What should we make of giants, trolls, enchanters, elves, dwarves, gnomes, and brownies?
- How can humans who are not members of one immediate family act together repeatedly as though they constitute some larger unit?
